⚠️
Early tenure is where churn lives — first 6 months drive most loss
New accounts churn at 4.8%/mo in months 0–6 versus 0.5%/mo after 5 years. Price (31%) and content gaps (22%) are the top stated reasons, with competitor switching (19%) to rival streamers close behind. Retention curves flatten sharply after month 4 — surviving the onboarding window is the single biggest lever.

Sport anchors the base; drama drives marathon engagement
Sports Diehards (24%) and Family Streamers (21%) are the two largest segments, but Premium Loyalists deliver the highest ARPU ($138) and tenure. Sport leads weekly viewing hours, while drama titles (The Outsider, Dynasty of Dragons) post the highest completion rates — the stickiest content for retention.
